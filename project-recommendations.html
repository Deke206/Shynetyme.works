LED PANEL RESEARCH · AUGUST 15, 2026

Useful panel projects and a better starting point near $100

Prices can change

For scrolling words, pixel art, weather, status boards and story animation, use a rigid HUB75 display panel. For a curved vehicle surface, wearable object or flexible installation, use a WS2812B flexible matrix. These are different electrical systems and should not share a controller without the correct interface.

BEST DISPLAY VALUE

Waveshare 64×64 P2.5 HUB75 panel

4096 RGB pixels, 160×160 mm, 5V/4A, adjustable brightness and chainable HUB75 input/output. This is the better route for text, graphics and animation. One panel is a low-cost prototype; four panels make a 128×128 display at roughly $104 for panels before controller and power.

  • Amazon panel listing
  • Amazon ESP32/HUB75 adapter
  • Amazon 5V/4A power supply

Reference panel price: $25.99 each. Check Amazon’s live price and availability.

CURVED / MOBILE

BTF-LIGHTING 16×16 flexible WS2812B matrix

256 individually addressable 5V pixels on a flexible 6.3-inch square board. Better for custom vehicle panels, bags, costumes and curved props than for readable text at a distance.

  • Amazon flexible matrix — observed $25.99
  • Amazon WLED controller — observed $34.90

Observed two-item subtotal: $60.89 before a correctly sized 5V supply and installation materials.

Bringin’ the Shyne back into the Nyte Tyme.

While we work each day and every night to survive the darkness, sometimes you have to become a beacon of Shynning lyte—safe, aware, and twinkling to your music whether you’re riding, driving, boating, or lighting your home.

ShyneTyme.Works brings creative works to the lyte, helping you direct your glow toward the right items and diodes to create a particular, unique set of LED lytes. Our services and growing product Project Power are built to shape the mood, environment, space, and mobile aura around you into a twinkling, sparkling, messaged Shyne.

So take a little Tyme to contemplate how that little lyte of yours—or your young one’s—can be brought out. And just maybe, you’ll want the Works!

# Public catalog business rules

- ShyneTyme supplies materials as part of the complete project quote.
- No Amazon links, affiliate links, ASINs, supplier prices or commission language appear in the public catalog.
- Outdoor recommendations require protected products such as IP65, IP66, IP67 or IP68.
- Bike recommendations prioritize 5V and compact/narrow products, with supported 12V options retained.
- Auto recommendations prioritize 12V products and protected exterior rows when exterior zones are selected.
- Home recommendations support 12V and 24V, with protected rows for exterior zones and covered or non-covered rows for interior zones.
- Exact catalog IDs remain attached through simulator suggestions and contact-page submission.
